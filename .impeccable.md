## Design Context

### Users

The primary user is a sports and prediction-market researcher reviewing one
NFL game at a time. Their job is to replay game state, inspect venue-specific
market paths, query candidate event/market relationships, and verify every
displayed claim against immutable lineage. The interface is a research
workspace, not a live trading terminal.

### Brand Personality

Evidence-first, exacting, and exploratory. The product should create confidence
through visible provenance and restrained presentation while still making
temporal patterns quick to notice.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use a light, industrial-editorial research aesthetic inspired by a well-made
game book and an analytical field notebook. Make the football field, event
timeline, and two-outcome market paths the memorable visual system. Avoid
neon-on-dark trading-terminal tropes, generic KPI-card grids, glassmorphism,
decorative sparklines, and invented sports imagery.

### Design Principles

1. Put replay and evidence before summary metrics.
2. Use team and venue color only when it carries meaning.
3. Reveal detail progressively: game, event, market, then audit lineage.
4. Keep observed, derived, unavailable, ambiguous, and contaminated states
   visually and textually distinct.
5. Preserve keyboard access, color-independent labels, reduced-motion support,
   and responsive access to every critical research control.
